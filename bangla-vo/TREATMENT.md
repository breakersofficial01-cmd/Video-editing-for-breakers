# বন্দি সবুজ দানা — The Green Prisoner (Chalkboard, 16:9)

Breaking Biology · 1920×1080 · 30 fps · Bangla · ~55 s
Voice: the owner's own Bangla recording (51.1 s), used as is, starting at 0.5 s.
Subtitles: Bangla only, burned in on the style's soft dark plate, also exported as `subtitles_bn.srt`.
Brief decisions: Chalkboard style; 16:9; the "৭০ কোটি" line is subtitled without a number
("কোটি কোটি বছর আগে…"); end card with the name "Breaking Biology" only.

## 1. Three candidate structures

A. **A single journey into the cell.** Wide classroom → microscope → into the eyepiece circle →
   cell → grains → bacterium. Clear, but it's the default "zoom into the cell" cliché and puts
   the misconception in the background.
B. **Misconception set right (write, wipe, rewrite).** The board writes what everyone believed
   ("কোষ নিজেই বানায়"), Schimper's observation wipes it, and the truth is rewritten in the same
   spot. Then a sliding panel reveals the "decades later" answer: a bacterium that walked into a
   cell and never left.
C. **Two hands in dialogue.** White chalk = the textbook; pale yellow = Schimper's notebook
   arguing back. Charming, but 51 s of voice leaves little room for a back-and-forth.

**Chosen: B, with C's second hand used once.** The voiceover is literally a misconception
corrected ("সবাই ভাবত… কিন্তু শিম্পার দেখলেন অন্য কিছু"), the chalkboard's native move.
Schimper's "unbelievable suspicion" is written in the pale yellow second hand, like his note.
The sliding panel answers "কয়েক দশক পর বিজ্ঞান বলল, তিনি ঠিক ছিলেন".

## 2. Logline and arc

A young botanist looks into a microscope in 1883, sees green grains dividing on their own, and
guesses they were once free living things; decades later science proves the prisoner is a
bacterium that still feeds the whole planet.

- **Setup (0–14 s):** 1883, Germany. A microscope in white chalk; its view circle holds a cell
  with small green grains (accent chalk). The belief is written beneath: "কোষ নিজেই বানায়?"
- **Turn (14–33 s):** one damp wipe removes the belief. In the same place: "নিজেরাই ভাগ হয়!",
  a grain drawn splitting in two. The name **ক্লোরোপ্লাস্ট** written large. Schimper's yellow
  note: "একসময় স্বাধীন জীব?"
- **Answer (33–47 s):** the front panel slides away. On the back board: a free green bacterium
  enters a larger cell and the gap closes behind it; a sun with rays, a leaf, "আলো → খাবার",
  a globe.
- **Ending (47–55 s):** pull back to the classroom wall, the lesson complete; "মুখস্থ নয় — গল্প"
  underlined once. End card: "Breaking Biology" written in chalk, one green grain dotting it.

The ending echoes the opening: the green grain that opened the film dots the brand name.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Learn from:** a good classroom storyteller's board work (few words, big letters, drawing grows
  exactly when named); popular science storytelling's "small observation → enormous
  consequence" pacing.
- **Don't take:** any channel's characters, palettes, logos or music; no textbook diagram
  copied; no institution's logo.

## 4. Look and the tracked component

- Slate: deep ink-green, wooden frame, chalk tray, warm wall and a clock, as the style asks.
- White chalk: microscope, cell outlines, words.
- **Accent chalk = leaf green**, reserved for the one thing the lesson tracks: grain →
  chloroplast → bacterium. It never draws arrows or underlines.
- **Second hand = pale yellow**: Schimper's suspicion only.
- Bangla on the board is drawn by the chalk engine stroke by stroke: centre lines traced from an
  OFL hand-lettered Bangla face (Atma), correctly shaped (conjuncts), ordered left to right with
  each word's মাত্রা (headline) drawn after its letters, as a hand writes Bangla. Never a font
  outline faded in. Numbers in Bangla digits (১৮৮৩).

## 7. Cue map

- 84 bpm (bar = 2.857 s). Music 15–18 dB under the voice.
- 0–13.4: sparse plucked guitar arpeggio (Am–F–C–G); a marimba tick as each green grain is born.
- 13.4–14.4: music out (silence before the turn); the wipe is the first sound after.
- 14.4–32.5: pizzicato bass enters; arpeggio a little brighter (C–G–Am–F).
- 32.5–33.8: one held note under the panel roll.
- 33.8–47.5: full but soft: guitar, marimba, bass, building to the sun.
- 47.5–51.8: one guitar line; a held chord on "গল্প".
- 51.8–55.5: end chord with a marimba ding on the green dot (L-cut over the whip).
